Research Experience

Degenerate Diffusions in Finite and Infinite Dimensions: Smoothing and Convergence

Student Researcher · Advisor: David P. Herzog

Summer 2026–Present

NSF-funded research · Award DMS-2246491

  • Develop quantitative finite-time Gaussian-approximation results for displacement in periodic Langevin dynamics.
  • Proved a one-dimensional Berry–Esseen estimate with Kolmogorov error bounded by C/√(γT), identifying γT as the effective relaxation scale.
  • Use measure-theoretic probability, Lp moment estimates, Markov-additive processes, semigroup mixing, martingale methods, and diffusion limits.
  • Implemented Python numerical experiments for Langevin dynamics and effective-diffusion estimation.

Nonlinear MBS Pricing Models

Student Researcher · Advisor: Rana D. Parshad

2026–Present
