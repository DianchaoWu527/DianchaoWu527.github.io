Research Experience

Degenerate Diffusions in Finite and Infinite Dimensions: Smoothing and Convergence

Student Researcher · Advisor: David P. Herzog

Summer 2026–Present

NSF-funded research · Award DMS-2246491

  • Formulated the Berry–Esseen framework for the unwrapped displacement and proved the overdamped results.
  • Proved the main underdamped theorem and supporting propositions via a direct Markov-additive treatment of the displacement; verification of Assumption (A2) was carried out by O. Smith.
  • Proved in Proposition 11.1 that, for one-dimensional periodic underdamped dynamics started from equilibrium and sufficiently small positive γ, the Berry–Esseen constant Cγ can be chosen with Cγ ≤ Cγ−1/2, where C is independent of γ.
  • Carried out the original numerical illustration and drafted Part I.

Numerical study of the low-friction regime: A. Valse.

Nonlinear MBS Pricing Models

Student Researcher · Advisor: Rana D. Parshad

2026–Present

  • Study uncertainty propagation through random-coefficient MBS pricing PDEs and ongoing extensions with fixed-p p-Laplacian diffusion.
  • Developed a working analysis of moment bounds, mean-square stability, and first-order sensitivity with a second-order remainder for bounded smooth prepayment perturbations in a reduced semilinear model.
  • Derived centered-noise mean and variance expansions and examined perturbation scaling through Fourier spectral simulations.
  • Built an initial backtesting workflow using real transaction data; empirical validation of nonlinear-diffusion modifications remains ongoing.
