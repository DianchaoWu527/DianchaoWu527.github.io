Current Research

Stochastic Dynamics and Mathematical Finance

My primary project develops Berry–Esseen bounds for periodic Langevin dynamics, including overdamped and underdamped results and the one-dimensional low-friction estimate in Proposition 11.1. A second ongoing project concerns prediction, backtesting, and stochastic sensitivity in nonlinear MBS pricing models.
